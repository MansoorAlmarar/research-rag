# Orbit Semiconductor, annual filing notes FY2025 (fictional)

Orbit Semiconductor is a fictional designer of chips for data centres, used here only as demo data.

## Customers

The two largest customers accounted for 61% of revenue: Customer A at 38% and Customer B at 23%. The loss of either would materially reduce revenue. No other customer exceeded 6%.

## Financials

Revenue was $3.4 billion, up 41%. Research and development spending was $310 million, or 9% of revenue. Gross margin was 64%. Net income was $820 million.

## Manufacturing

Orbit does not own factories. It relies on one foundry partner in East Asia for 92% of its wafers and a second source for the rest. A new supply agreement secures capacity through 2028 with prepayments of $400 million.

## Capital returns

The board authorised a $500 million share repurchase programme and the company bought back $180 million of stock during the year. It does not pay a dividend.

## Risks

Export controls could restrict sales of its top product to some regions, which accounted for 17% of revenue. Competitors are releasing rival products in the next two years. The company also faces a patent dispute with a former supplier, with no damages estimate disclosed.
