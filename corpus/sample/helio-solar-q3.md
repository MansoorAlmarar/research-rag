# Helio Solar, Q3 FY2026 results (fictional)

Helio Solar is a fictional manufacturer of solar panels and inverters used here only as demo data.

## Headline numbers

Revenue for the quarter was $412 million, up 18% from $349 million a year earlier. Gross margin was 31.4%, compared with 28.9% in the same quarter last year. Net income was $29 million, or $0.41 per diluted share.

## Segments

The utility segment produced $251 million of revenue, up 24%. The residential segment produced $161 million, up 9%. Residential growth slowed because higher interest rates reduced home installations in the southern region.

## Guidance

Management raised full-year revenue guidance to between $1.58 billion and $1.62 billion. The previous range was $1.52 billion to $1.58 billion. Gross margin guidance for the year is 30% to 32%.

## Cash and capital spending

Cash and equivalents ended the quarter at $344 million. Capital expenditure was $58 million, mostly for the new inverter line in Texas, which is expected to start production in the second quarter of FY2027. Total debt is $220 million.

## Risks

Polysilicon prices rose 11% during the quarter and the company buys about 70% of its supply on short contracts. A tariff review on imported cells could raise costs from January. The company also notes that one utility customer accounts for 14% of backlog.
