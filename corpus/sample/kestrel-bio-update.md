# Kestrel Bio, business update Q2 (fictional)

Kestrel Bio is a fictional clinical-stage biotechnology company used here only as demo data. It has no product revenue.

## Cash

Cash and short-term investments were $188 million at the end of the quarter. Net cash used in operations was $26 million for the quarter. Management estimates this funds operations for about 22 months, into the first quarter of 2028.

## Lead program

The lead program is KB-204, a once-daily oral treatment for moderate psoriasis. The Phase 2 trial enrolled 240 patients across 38 sites. The primary endpoint is the share of patients reaching a 75% improvement in the standard severity score at week 16. Topline results are expected in the second quarter of 2027.

## Pipeline

KB-310, a preclinical program for an inflammatory bowel condition, is expected to file for clinical trial approval in the first half of 2027. A third program, KB-115, was paused this quarter to save cash.

## Partnership

Kestrel has a collaboration with Marlow Pharma covering KB-204 outside North America. Marlow paid $40 million upfront and may pay up to $310 million in milestones plus tiered royalties. Kestrel keeps all rights in North America.

## Risks

A negative Phase 2 result would remove most of the company's value. The company has 94 employees and relies on two contract manufacturers. It may need to raise money before the Phase 2 readout if enrolment costs run over budget.
