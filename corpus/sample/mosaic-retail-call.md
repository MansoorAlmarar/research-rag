# Mosaic Retail, Q1 earnings call excerpt (fictional)

Mosaic Retail is a fictional apparel and home goods retailer used here only as demo data. The text below is a short, invented call excerpt.

## Prepared remarks

The chief executive said comparable store sales rose 3.1% in the quarter, the first positive quarter in a year. Total sales were $2.34 billion. E-commerce was 27% of sales, up from 23%. The company operates 640 stores after closing 12 and opening 4 during the quarter.

## Inventory

The chief financial officer said inventory ended the quarter up 9% versus last year, while sales grew 5%. She said most of the extra stock is spring seasonal goods and the company expects to clear it with promotions in the second quarter, which will weigh on margin by about 80 basis points.

## Margins

Gross margin was 38.6%, down 40 basis points. Selling and administrative costs were 31.2% of sales. Operating margin was 7.4%.

## Questions from analysts

An analyst asked about tariffs. The chief executive said about 22% of products are sourced from countries facing higher duties, and that the company would move half of that volume to other suppliers by the end of the year. Another analyst asked about store closures. Management said another 20 to 25 closures are planned this year, all of them stores with leases that end within 18 months.

## Outlook

The company reaffirmed full-year sales growth of 3% to 4% and expects operating margin of 7.0% to 7.6%. It did not give a quarterly forecast.
