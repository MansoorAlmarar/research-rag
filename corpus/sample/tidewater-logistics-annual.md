# Tidewater Logistics, annual report excerpt FY2025 (fictional)

Tidewater Logistics is a fictional container shipping and port services group used here only as demo data.

## Results

Revenue was $1.9 billion, up 4% on the prior year. Operating margin was 7.2%, down from 9.1%, because fuel and port fees rose faster than freight rates. Net income was $96 million.

## Fleet

The group operates 214 vessels, of which 151 are owned and 63 are chartered. The average vessel age is 11 years. Two vessels were retired this year and six new dual-fuel vessels are on order for delivery in 2027.

## Balance sheet

Total debt is $1.1 billion and equity is $1.22 billion, a debt to equity ratio of 0.9. Interest cover was 4.6 times. The board kept the annual dividend at $0.60 per share.

## Costs

Fuel was 29% of operating costs, up from 25%. Hedges cover about 40% of expected fuel use for the next twelve months. Port fees rose 8% after two terminal operators renegotiated contracts.

## Risks

Freight rates on the Asia to Europe route fell 12% in the second half. A strike at a major European port cost the group an estimated $14 million. New emissions rules from 2028 will require retrofitting older vessels, with a budget of $210 million set aside.
